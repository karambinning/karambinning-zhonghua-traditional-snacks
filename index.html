What we make

A short menu, made properly.

小吃 · xiǎochī
01
Dumplings 饺子

Folded by hand at the counter and cooked to order — boiled until plump, or pan-fried until the bottoms crack golden. Served by the dozen, the way they ought to be.

02
Noodles 面条

Cooked to order and served in clear broth or tossed dry with sauce — the kind of bowl that fills you for the afternoon. Chewy in the middle, soft at the ends.

03
Filled buns 包子

Soft white dough wrapped around savoury fillings, steamed in stacked bamboo. Eat them with your hands while they're hot.

04
Traditional snacks 小吃

A short rotation of the smaller dishes a snack shop is meant to do well — the kind you'd find at a stall in a Beijing back-lane or a Shanghai morning market. Ask at the counter.

A snack shop. Not a restaurant. No reservations, no tasting menu — just food made the same way it's been made for a long time.
